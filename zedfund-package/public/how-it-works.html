How ZedFund Works

No bank account, no card — just your phone. Here's how Zambians fund and support real community projects.

📝
1. Create

Share your story, set a funding goal, and launch your campaign in minutes — no paperwork required to start.

🔗
2. Share

Reach your community through social media, WhatsApp, and SMS. Every campaign gets a shareable link.

📲
3. Receive

Donors pay instantly with MTN, Airtel, or Zamtel mobile money. Withdraw your funds the same way.

For Donors

  • Browse verified campaigns by category, province, or funding goal.
  • Enter your mobile number and approve the payment request on your phone — no card needed.
  • Track exactly how much has been raised and follow campaign updates.

For Organizers

  • Submit your campaign with documents for identity and project verification.
  • Once approved, your campaign goes live and starts accepting mobile money donations.
  • Withdraw available funds straight to your MTN, Airtel, or Zamtel account.
